Opportunity Red Flags

The following signs may indicate that an internship or career opportunity requires additional verification.

1. Upfront payment:
Requests for money to obtain an internship, job, interview, training position, equipment, or guaranteed employment should be treated as a major warning sign.

2. Unrealistic promises:
Claims of extremely high income, guaranteed employment, guaranteed promotion, or extraordinary benefits with little experience required should be investigated carefully.

3. Missing company information:
An opportunity that does not clearly identify the company, organization, website, location, or contact information has increased uncertainty.

4. Vague job description:
A description with no clear responsibilities, requirements, or expected work may indicate poor quality or increased risk.

5. Pressure tactics:
Urgent demands to apply immediately, pay immediately, or provide sensitive information without sufficient explanation are warning signs.

6. Suspicious communication:
Unofficial email addresses, unusual contact methods, inconsistent company information, or requests to communicate exclusively through personal accounts should be verified.

7. Sensitive information requests:
Applicants should be cautious when an opportunity requests unnecessary sensitive personal, financial, or identity information during an early application stage.

8. No clear interview or selection process:
A company offering a position without any meaningful evaluation or explanation of the selection process should be investigated.

9. Unclear compensation:
Missing compensation information is not automatically a scam, but the applicant should ask for clarification before accepting the opportunity.

10. Excessive unpaid work:
An opportunity requiring substantial work before selection, especially without clear educational value or explanation, should be evaluated carefully.

Important:
A red flag does not automatically prove that an opportunity is fraudulent. The system should identify risks and recommend verification rather than making unsupported accusations.
